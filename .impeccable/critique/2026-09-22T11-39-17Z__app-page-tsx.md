## Design Specificity Verdict

**LLM assessment**:
The interface exhibits exceptional craft discipline, firmly anchored in its "Terminal Readout" identity. It feels like precision measuring instrumentation—reminiscent of Dieter Rams' Braun hardware, Teenage Engineering synthesizers, or a Bloomberg terminal—rather than another gamified consumer calorie tracker. There are no rainbow rings, confetti bursts, or moralizing streaks. Numerical data takes absolute precedence over decorative chrome. Recent updates from commit `d7194d3` dramatically reinforced product truth: over-budget meters now dynamically re-scale to 100% of the overage with a hairline vernier tick at the goal boundary rather than clipping, and row deletion requires an armed second tap on `sure?`. ## Overall Impression

A remarkably sharp, dignified, and tactile tool designed for fast, frictionless logging. It respects the user's intelligence and time. The recent addition of physical vernier ticks on over-budget meters and two-step delete arming elevates its ergonomic safety. The primary opportunity lies in calming the visual noise: softening the aggressive black Log Food header and fusing the Calorie readout with secondary macro meters into a singular, unified instrument panel.

## What's Working

1. **The Tactical Vernier Meter & Over-Budget Cues (`components/Meter.tsx`)**:
   Rescaling the bar to 100% of consumed value while placing a 1px physical tick mark at the target boundary resolves a classic data-viz failure mode where overages are invisibly clamped. It conveys over-budget state geometrically and typographically (`+15.2 over`) without relying solely on color.
2. **Two-Step Inline Deletion Arming (`components/EntryRow.tsx`)**:
   Replacing `✕` with an armed uppercase `sure?` in `var(--over)` red for 4 seconds, buffered by a vertical hairline from the `Copy` button, protects thumb-zone interactions while preserving single-row speed without heavy modal friction.
3. **Time-Decayed Contextual Quick Add (`lib/quick-add-rank.ts`)**:
   Weighting meal-specific frequency against a 14-day exponential decay automatically surfaces breakfast staples at 8:00 and dinner staples at 19:00, making 1-tap logging instantly accessible throughout the day.

## Priority Issues

### [P1] Visual Hierarchy Conflict: Solid-Black Log Food Header vs. Calorie Hero Readout
- **Why it matters**: The inverted solid-black `LOG FOOD` header (`min-h-[56px] bg-ink text-panel`) creates an intense visual gravity well at the top of the viewport that overpowers the primary calorie display directly beneath it. The screen feels like an accordion form placed atop a data instrument.
- **Fix**: Soften the collapsed Log Food header to standard panel styling (`bg-panel-2 border border-line text-ink`), reserving solid inverted `bg-ink` exclusively for conclusive primary actions (e.g., submit buttons or the selected day indicator).
- **Suggested command**: `/impeccable layout`

### [P1] Visual Container Fatigue ("Panel Soup") & Nested Cards on Mobile
- **Why it matters**: On a 390px mobile screen, the user is confronted with 7-8 separate 1px bordered boxes stacked vertically: Week strip, Log Food, Quick Add input, Quick Add chips, Calories card, Nutrition grid, and Weight card. In addition, the runtime detector flagged nested cards inside the AddFood modal. This visual packaging creates visual monotony and high extraneous cognitive load.
- **Fix**: Consolidate Calories and Nutrition into a single unified telemetry panel. Eliminate redundant outer borders, using subtle tonal shifts (`bg-panel-2`) and hairline dividers (`divide-line-soft`) for internal separation.
- **Suggested command**: `/impeccable distill`

### [P2] Dialog Text Viewport Edge Bleed & Leading Constraints
- **Why it matters**: The runtime detector identified helper copy in the AddFood manual dialog bleeding to 13px from screen edges (under the 16px mobile safety boundary), and 11px week chips sitting at 1.27x leading. On narrow devices, cramped margins reduce legibility and feel unpolished.
- **Fix**: Enforce minimum 16px padding on modal content containers (`px-4`), and tune week strip typography to at least 1.35x leading (`leading-snug`).
- **Suggested command**: `/impeccable polish`

### [P2] Micronutrient Noise for Unconfigured Goals
- **Why it matters**: Users who only track calories or calories + protein are permanently shown zeroed hairline tracks for Fiber, Sugar, and Sodium. These unconfigured metrics consume vertical space and draw attention to missing data.
- **Fix**: Only render secondary micronutrients if target bounds are configured in Settings, or hide them under a quiet inline disclosure toggle (`+ 3 more nutrients`).
- **Suggested command**: `/impeccable quieter`

## Questions to Consider

- *What if the Calorie Readout and Secondary Macros were fused into a single unified telemetry cluster?* Fusing the 2.25rem calorie display and the 6 macro meters inside one bordered instrument face would eliminate panel fatigue and create a dramatic, cohesive focal point.
- *Could the "As Eaten" vs "Per 100g" modal switch be inferred automatically from unit selection?* Selecting `g` or `ml` against a label could automatically engage scaling, removing the intimidating database concept from the user's conscious workflow.
- *What if unconfigured micronutrients were collapsed by default?* Displaying only calories and protein by default would drastically reduce vertical height on mobile and highlight what actually matters.
