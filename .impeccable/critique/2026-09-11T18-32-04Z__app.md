## Overall Impression

A serious, opinionated tool that respects its user's intelligence and time. The instrument-panel aesthetic is distinctive and earned. The architecture is impressively robust — offline-first, undo everywhere, data integrity enforced at the scaling layer. Single biggest opportunity: the AddFood composer's conceptual complexity.

---

## What's Working

1. **Unapologetic density.** High information density made legible through monospace fonts, strict grids, and careful hierarchy.
2. **Offline-first resilience.** Offline snapshots, stale-data warnings, and draft saves — best-in-class for a self-hosted PWA.
3. **Data integrity mechanics.** Scaling engine refuses to silently log corrupted data.

---

## Priority Issues

### [P1] The Math Burden in AddFood
The distinction between "portion", "per 100g/ml", and "unitless" leaks structural complexity into the core logging flow. This is the core loop — 4-8 times/day. Fix: collapse scaling behind input, default to as-eaten, surface assistant as escape hatch.
**Suggested command:** /impeccable clarify

### [P2] Dense Inline Editing in EntryRow
Edit state expands into a large form requiring choice between "Change portion" and "Correct values" — a conceptual distinction requiring understanding of entry provenance. Fix: merge edit modes, auto-detect based on provenance.
**Suggested command:** /impeccable distill

### [P3] First-Run Emptiness
No onboarding, no empty-state guidance. Help and Documentation scores 2/4. Fix: meaningful empty states that teach by doing.
**Suggested command:** /impeccable onboard

### [P2] No Bulk-Add Path
Logging multiple items requires repeated trips through AddFood. Fix: multi-select in Recent/Favorites with batch logging.
**Suggested command:** /impeccable shape

## Persona Red Flags

**Alex (Power User):** No multi-select for batch logging; copy tray requires sequential paste.
**Jordan (First-Timer):** AddFood math engine is intimidating; "unitless" vs "per 100g" has no frame of reference; no warmth or encouragement; "TDEE" is unexplained jargon.
**Casey (Distracted Mobile User):** EntryRow edit state is a tap-target minefield while walking; AddFood unit/amount fields are small and close together.
