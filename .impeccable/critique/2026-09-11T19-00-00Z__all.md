## Overall Impression

Diet Tracker achieves a rare, refreshing degree of mechanical integrity and aesthetic restraint. It feels like an authentic telemetry instrument rather than a commercial engagement trap. The foundation is rock solid, but on mobile portrait viewports the interface suffers from vertical congestion — primarily because the expanded `AddFood` composer monopolizes the screen and buries the actual meal log.

## What's Working

1. **Mathematical Rigor and Data Integrity**: Food quantities are formally quoted against references (`portion`, `per 100g/ml`, `unitless`). Switching units restates amounts mathematically (e.g. 241g → 8.5oz) rather than corrupting values, and density is never guessed.
2. **Bulletproof Offline and Draft Resilience**: Scoped offline snapshots (`offline-cache.ts`), date/user-scoped draft persistence (`food-draft.ts`), and wake revalidation ensure zero data loss during kitchen Wi-Fi drops or mobile tab suspensions.
3. **Pervasive, Non-Blocking Undo Architecture**: Every destructive or state-altering action (deleting entries, clearing meals, modifying goals, unpinning favorites) executes instantly without modal friction, backed by a persistent, dependable Undo toast.

## Priority Issues

### [P1] Vertical Stack Congestion: Always-Open AddFood Displaces Daily Log
- **Why it matters**: On mobile portrait screens, the expanded `AddFood` panel consumes over 450px of vertical space. Users logging a meal cannot see the entries they just logged or review their meal breakdown without extensive scrolling past 11 form inputs and two chip carousels.
- **Fix**: Default `showAdd` to a compact quick-entry bar that expands into full macro fields on demand, or implement a lightweight bottom drawer / modal sheet pattern for the full composer on mobile viewports.
- **Suggested command**: `/impeccable layout`

### [P1] Zero Visual Affordance for Entry Editing on Touch Devices
- **Why it matters**: In `EntryRow.tsx`, editing is triggered by tapping the food name (`entry-identity`). While desktop provides `hover:text-accent`, mobile touch devices offer zero visual cue that the text is interactive. Users either assume entries are read-only or repeatedly misclick the adjacent `Copy` and `Delete` icons.
- **Fix**: Add a subtle inline affordance (e.g. a micro-chevron, edit pencil icon, or an active touch feedback state) so the editable affordance is evident without relying on hover states.
- **Suggested command**: `/impeccable clarify`

### [P2] Inadvertently Punitive "Over" Calorie State Signaling
- **Why it matters**: Exceeding a daily caloric goal instantly flips the entire calorie figure and meter fill to `--over` red (`#b91c1c` / `#f87171`), identical to network failures and destructive deletion states. For lifters on a mass-building phase or individuals having a planned refeed, this communicates an erroneous "system failure" rather than an objective telemetry readout.
- **Fix**: Decouple target surplus from error red. Use a neutral indicator (e.g. an amber overfill hatch or distinct surplus marker) or introduce a goal type preference (deficit vs maintenance vs surplus).
- **Suggested command**: `/impeccable quieter`

### [P2] Visual & Structural Schism in WorkoutCard
- **Why it matters**: The `WorkoutCard` and `WorkoutSummaryCard` components introduce colorful icon sets (`Flame`, `Sparkles`, `Layers`), rounded card containers, and view-mode toggles that break the unified flat-hairline, tabular-monospace discipline of the rest of the application.
- **Fix**: Refactor the workout card into the strict terminal readout language: replace decorative icons with minimal monospace labels, flatten containers into 1px bordered panels, and unify workout metrics with the `.num` tabular system.
- **Suggested command**: `/impeccable distill`

## Persona Red Flags

- **Alex (Impatient Power User)**: AddFood forces tabbing through 11 individual input boxes (Name, Calories, Protein, Carbs, Fat, Amount, Unit, Meal, plus trace fields) just to reach the Log button unless they already know to press Enter. Furthermore, logging multi-item meals requires submitting and clearing fields one item at a time with no rapid batch text entry.
- **Jordan (Confused First-Timer)**: The toggle `AS EATEN` vs `PER 100 G` and the line *"Quoting per 100 · scales with amount below"* present technical jargon that creates hesitation when logging home-cooked food. Additionally, tapping the `⧉` copy button silently stages the item into an unseen tray and opens the composer rather than duplicating the row, leading Jordan to believe the action failed.
- **Casey (Distracted Mobile User)**: Holding a phone one-handed in a busy kitchen, Casey experiences frequent tap collisions: the 13px Delete `✕` button is separated by only 8px from Copy `⧉`. Moreover, tapping a recent food chip logs the entry, but because the composer occupies the viewport, the logged item is invisible below the fold, leaving Casey uncertain whether the tap registered.

## Minor Observations

- The barcode scanner icon (`ScanBarcode`) in `AddFood` is subtle and easily missed beside the search input.
- The unlogged meals prompt (`+ breakfast`, `+ lunch`) sits at the very bottom of all logged meals, disconnected from the top meal selector.
- `ShortcutsModal` uses an ASCII `×` close glyph rather than the bundled Lucide `X` icon, diverging from icon conventions.
- Goals inputs allow values up to `1000000` without soft sanity guardrails.
- The active tab indicator in `BottomNav` relies purely on stroke weight (1.75 to 2.25) and amber color, which can wash out in harsh daylight.
