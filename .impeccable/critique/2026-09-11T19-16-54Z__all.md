## Design Specificity Verdict

**Verdict: Deeply Grounded & Authentically Realized.**

The visual and interaction architecture of Diet Tracker is inextricably tied to its stated North Star: **"The Terminal Readout."** It deliberately rejects the ubiquitous tropes of modern consumer fitness software — there are no pastel gradients, no glowing circular progress rings, no congratulatory confetti or mascot animations, and no streak-shaming mechanics.

Instead, the interface functions as an uncompromising, high-density telemetry console:
- **Semantic Typography Split**: A strict, uncompromised division of labor. System monospace tabular numerals (`.num`, `tnum 1`, `-0.01em`) speak exclusively for measured physical data (calories, grams, dates, reps, weights, timestamps, connector URLs). System sans speaks for human-authored text (food names, notes, instructional copy).
- **The One Signal Color Rule**: Single amber accent (`--accent`: `#b45309` light / `#f59e0b` dark) signals active state, in-range progress, and inline creation affordances (`★ favorite`, `⬚ save label`). Green (`--ok`) is held strictly in reserve for remaining headroom, and red (`--over`) solely indicates exceeding target or destructive intent.
- **Cross-Surface Fidelity**:
  - **Food Tab**: 7-day strip with monospace dates and 2px progress rails; calorie hero with a faint 16px engineering grid (`.gridlines`); 6 macro horizontal meters with ease-out width animations; contextual daily weight logging instrument; and structured meal groups.
  - **Workout Tab**: Brings the "Obsidian markdown" note philosophy directly into lifting, paired with an instrument telemetry toolbar (Volume, Sets, Reps), exercise set count badges, and an integrated 90s rest timer.
  - **Settings Tab**: Avoids settings bloat by chunking daily targets into primary macros, folding micronutrients into progressive disclosure, nesting the TDEE calculator, and treating the MCP connector URL as a secured bearer credential with masked display and explicit reveal/copy controls.

## What's Working

1. **Unflinching "Terminal Readout" Design Discipline**:
   The app achieves exceptional clarity by treating data as the sole content. The strict split between monospace tabular figures for physical quantities and system sans for human text, framed by 1px hairlines and near-monochrome surfaces, delivers an authentic, clutter-free utility.
2. **True Dual-Front-Door Architecture (UI & MCP)**:
   Symmetrical parity between human touch input and AI agent capture. Provenance metadata (`ai` indicator, `productId`, scaling basis) guarantees that entries generated by an assistant or logged manually are equally inspectable, editable, and trustworthy.
3. **Defensive Mobile-First Engineering**:
   The 16px iOS font floor completely prevents Safari viewport auto-zoom without disabling pinch-to-zoom. Real padding on `.glyph-btn` eliminates tap collisions. `scroll-mb-28` prevents keyboard overlap, and offline snapshotting ensures instant, dependable utility in kitchen or gym settings.
