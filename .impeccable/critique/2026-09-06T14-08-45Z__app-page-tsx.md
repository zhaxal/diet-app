Design specificity and strengths:
- The day-based structure, tabular figures, and restrained amber signal suit a personal nutrition ledger. Preserve this identity.
- Add food explicitly models quantities, constrains incompatible units, and previews scaled nutrition.
- Sync timestamps, offline messaging, and assistant provenance support two ways of maintaining one record.

Priority issues:

1. [P1] Date selection and displayed records can disagree. loadDay leaves the previous entries visible while fetching, then accepts responses without checking that their date is still selected. Rapid navigation can let an older response overwrite the current day. Bind data to its date, show a loading state when they differ, and ignore superseded requests. Evidence: app/page.tsx:232 and :342. Command: $impeccable harden. Source-confirmed; network race not reproduced live.

2. [P1] Editing an amount does not rescale nutrition. Changing 200 g to 100 g in EntryRow retains the calories and macros; changing g to oz retains the numeric amount. Add food behaves differently. Distinguish portion changes from raw corrections and reuse the composer's conversion and preview logic for portion changes. Evidence: components/EntryRow.tsx:53, :115; components/AddFood.tsx:424; app/api/entries/[id]/route.ts:49. Commands: $impeccable clarify and $impeccable harden.

3. [P1] Information density shrinks controls and obscures content on narrow screens. At 390×844, the sodium value 120/2300mg extends to x383.31 beyond its cell edge x364.99 and panel edge approximately x378. Meal selectors measure 30px tall, COPY ALL 14px, and AS EATEN/PER 100 toggles 22px. The oatmeal row truncates its name and hides its recorded 200 g while retaining time, calories, copy, and delete. Give interactive boxes a real 44px minimum on coarse pointers without overlapping neighbors; stack meter labels over figures or change the narrow grid, and reserve space for the portion beside the food name. Evidence: app/globals.css:101, :149, :236; app/page.tsx:853, :879; components/Meter.tsx:25; components/EntryRow.tsx:165. Command: $impeccable adapt. Overflow and truncation were observed independently; control heights were measured in desktop-pointer viewport emulation.

4. [P2] Closing Add food discards unfinished work. Collapsing the panel unmounts its locally stored draft; changing tabs also removes it. Keep the draft outside the panel, scoped to account/day/meal, and offer explicit discard. Evidence: app/page.tsx:1203; components/AddFood.tsx:135. Command: $impeccable harden.

5. [P2] Recovery is fleeting and not announced. Toasts lack live-region semantics; Undo disappears after six seconds without pausing during interaction. Announce feedback appropriately and keep actionable recovery available long enough to use. Evidence: components/Toast.tsx:34 and :42. Command: $impeccable harden.

Persona red flags:

- Frequent user: rapid date navigation and portion corrections can produce misleading readings.
